Privacy Policy

Daily Insights Mood Tracker · Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Daily Insights Mood Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. This includes, but is not limited to: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal identifiers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All information you enter in the App (your moods, activities, and preferences) is stored locally on your device only using the browser's built-in storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device, is never sent to any server, and is deleted if you uninstall the App or clear your browser data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services for subscription payments:

  • PayPal — for processing subscription payments. Their privacy policy is available at paypal.com/privacy.

We do not share any of your personal data with these services beyond what is required to complete a transaction you initiate.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or fingerprinting technologies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
